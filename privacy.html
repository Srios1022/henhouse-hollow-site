Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Henhouse Hollow is a farming game for iPhone made by Sergio Rios ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses. In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and your farm is saved only on your device.

Information stored on your device

Your farm progress (coins, gems, crops, animals, settings) is saved on your iPhone. We don't receive it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

In-app purchases

Gem packs are optional and are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your name, Apple ID, card or billing details. Apple's privacy policy is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

To check and restore purchases, the game uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID created by the app, your purchase history (which packs, when, and the price), and basic device information such as device model, iOS version, app version and country or region. It's used only to make purchases work and to give us totals of sales. It isn't linked to your identity and isn't used for advertising or tracking. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or log-ins.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.

Children

The game doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases go through Apple, so parents can control them with Screen Time and "Ask to Buy".

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
